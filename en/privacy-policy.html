Golden Guns 3D – Privacy Policy

Publisher: Outworld Interactive

Contact: outworldinteractive@gmail.com

Effective date: 29 October 2026

App: Golden Guns 3D (Android, package name com.GoldenGuns3Dapp)

Why this policy exists

This text explains in plain language what information the game Golden Guns 3D collects, why it collects it, who can reach it, and how you can have it deleted. The game is currently in pre-alpha, which means it is still being developed and this policy may change along with it. Whenever we change the policy we update the effective date above, and if a change directly affects your data we also announce it inside the game.

If you play offline, without an account

If you choose Offline mode you play against bots or with friends on the same local network, and the game does not send any personal information to our cloud services. In this mode the player name, avatar colour, optional profile photo and all your settings stay on your phone only. When you uninstall the game, all of it is erased.

What is collected when you sign in with Google

To join online matches, add friends and form parties you need to sign in with your Google account. Sign-in goes through Google Play Games. During sign-in Google gives us a Google Play Games player ID that is specific to your account and the display name that will be used in the game. Google does not share your e-mail address, your Google profile photo, your contacts or anything else from your Google account with us, and we do not ask for them. The Google Play Games identity is handed to Unity Technologies' Unity Authentication service through a one-time verification code, and Unity creates a Unity player ID and a short-lived access token for your account in return. That ID and token are used to identify your account to our cloud services and to the game servers. The game server checks the token on every connection and rebuilds your name and friend ID itself instead of trusting what the connecting game reports.

After you sign in, a player profile is created. It contains your display name, an avatar colour, a friend ID that we give you (it starts with GG and is followed by six characters) and, only if you choose to add one, a small profile photo that you pick from your phone's gallery and crop. This profile is stored in Unity Cloud Save and can be read by other signed-in players, so that they can see you in the friends list, on the team bar and on the scoreboard. For this reason we recommend that you do not put anything in your name or photo that reveals who you are, where you live or other personal details.

When you use the friends feature, your friend list, the friend requests you send and receive, the players you block and your online status are kept in Unity's Friends service. Your status is shown to your friends as Online, In a match or Offline. When you create or join a party or squad, the fact that you are in that party is shared with the other members through Unity Multiplayer Services.

When you enter an online match, the game sends your character's position, your shots, kills and scores to the game server that runs the match. This information is needed for the match to work and is not stored anywhere after the match ends. The server also writes technical log files that contain your display name, your friend ID, the time you connected and your IP address. We keep these logs to run the servers, to find bugs and to stop abuse, and we delete them automatically after seven days.

Information that stays on your phone or is used locally

For local network (LAN) games, when you host a LAN match the game announces your phone's device name (for example "Alex's phone") on the Wi-Fi network you are connected to. The purpose is that friends on the same network can find your match in the list. Only devices on the same network can see this announcement and it is not sent over the internet. If your device name contains your real name, please be aware that other people on that network may see it.

Your preferences, such as graphics quality, frame rate, controls, language and your "I have read and agree" ticks, are stored only on your phone. The heat protection feature reads the thermal status that Android provides in order to lower the frame rate and image quality when your phone starts to overheat. That information never leaves your phone.

Microphone and notification permissions

The app tells Android that it may use the microphone permission for a voice chat feature we plan to add in the future. As of today the game does not record audio, does not listen to your microphone and does not send any audio anywhere. Android asks you separately whenever an app wants to use the microphone, and we will update this policy and tell you before voice chat is released. In the same way, the app declares the notification permission for possible future use, but today it does not send you any notification.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your location, your contact list, the files on your phone, camera images, audio recordings or your advertising ID. There are no ads in the game, no in-app purchases, and the game does not contain any analytics or crash-reporting software that tracks what you do.

Who handles your data on our behalf

For sign-in and the player ID we use Google's services (Google Play Games Services and Google sign-in), and how Google handles that data is described in the policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy. The infrastructure that stores your profile, your friends and your party is Unity Technologies' Unity Authentication, Cloud Save, Friends and Multiplayer Services, whose policy is at https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy. To connect players to our game servers we use a tunnel service called playit.gg, which can see the IP addresses of players who connect to a server. The game servers themselves run on equipment operated by Outworld Interactive. We do not sell your information, we do not share it with advertisers, and we do not pass it to anyone other than the parties named above.

How long we keep information

Your profile, friend information and account stay in the cloud until you delete your account. Server logs are deleted after seven days. Information that Google and Unity keep in their own systems follows those companies' policies.

Security

Connections to Google and Unity services use encrypted HTTPS connections. The connection between the game and a game server during a match, on the other hand, is not encrypted at the moment. We plan to improve this in later versions and we want to be honest about it. No system is completely secure, which is why it matters that you do not put personal details in your profile name or photo and that you do not share your account with others.

Your rights and deleting your data

You can delete your account and your data at any time. Inside the game, open Settings, go to the Account tab and press "Delete account". This erases your cloud profile, your photo, your friend relations and your account, and it also clears the related data on your phone. If you only want to remove your photo you can do that in the profile editor. If you can no longer access the game, you can also reach us by e-mail as described on our account deletion page.

You can send requests for access to, correction of or deletion of your data to outworldinteractive@gmail.com. If you live in Türkiye you have the rights listed in Article 11 of the Personal Data Protection Law no. 6698 (KVKK), which include learning whether your data is processed, asking for information about it, asking for it to be corrected or deleted and applying to the Personal Data Protection Board. If you live in the European Union, the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom you have the rights granted by the GDPR and the right to complain to your local data protection authority. We try to answer requests within a reasonable time and in any case within thirty days.

Children

Golden Guns 3D is a game with armed combat and is not directed at children under thirteen. If the law of your country sets a higher age, for example sixteen in some countries, that age applies. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below that age. If you notice that a child has created an account, please write to us and we will delete it.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page and the effective date at the top shows which version applies. For questions, requests and complaints you can reach Outworld Interactive at outworldinteractive@gmail.com.
