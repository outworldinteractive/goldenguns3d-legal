Golden Guns 3D – Deleting Your Account and Data

App: Golden Guns 3D (com.GoldenGuns3Dapp), published by Outworld Interactive

Deleting your account from inside the game

The fastest way to delete your account is the game itself. Open the game and sign in with your Google account, then go to Settings from the main menu and open the Account tab. Under that tab you will find a "Delete account" row. When you tap the "Delete" button in that row the game shows a confirmation window that tells you the action cannot be undone. Once you confirm, the deletion starts and takes a few seconds. You need an internet connection for this. If there is no connection the game tells you so and deletes nothing, so a half-finished deletion cannot happen.

The deletion removes everything the game keeps about you in the cloud. This includes your profile (your display name, avatar colour, the friend ID we gave you and, if you added one, your profile photo), your friend list, the friend requests you sent and received, the players you blocked, and the account itself, which means your Unity player ID. At the same time the profile information, photo copy, account choice and "I have read and agree" ticks on your phone are cleared. When it is done you are signed out and returned to the sign-in screen. If you later sign in with Google again, you get a brand new, empty account; your old friends and profile do not come back.

If you can no longer open the game: deleting by e-mail

If you have uninstalled the game or can no longer reach your phone, you can send your deletion request by e-mail. Write to outworldinteractive@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Golden Guns 3D account" and tell us in your message your friend ID (the six-character ID that starts with GG and is shown in the game under Settings, Account) or the display name you used in the game. If you do not remember your ID and you are able to reinstall the game, the easiest way is to reinstall it, sign in and delete the account with the first method. We process requests that arrive by e-mail within thirty days at the latest, and we reply to you when it is done.

What is not deleted and data held by others

The game server's technical log files can contain your display name, friend ID, connection time and IP address. Those logs exist to run the servers and find bugs, and they are deleted automatically after seven days, independently of an account deletion.

Your Google account and your Google Play Games profile belong to Google and are not under our control; the information Google holds there is covered by Google's policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Deleting your account in the game does not delete your Google account. If you played in Offline mode, the information from that mode exists only on your phone and is removed when you uninstall the game.

Questions

For any question about deletion or your data you can write to outworldinteractive@gmail.com. For more about how we handle data, please also read our Privacy Policy.
