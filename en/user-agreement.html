Online play, ranked matches and fair play

Online play has an age limit. The game asks once for the year you were born and checks it when you connect; the limit is thirteen years, or higher where the law of your country says so. You must tell the truth: an account whose owner gave a false year, or who is below the limit, may be blocked from online play.

Ranked matches are played five against five without bots, with fixed rules. Your rating (MMR), rank, level and experience points are numbers inside the game; they have no monetary value, give you no right to anything outside the game, and we may change how they are calculated, correct them after a bug, or move them part of the way back to the starting rating when a new season begins (every ninety days). In a ranked match you are expected to confirm that you are ready when a match is found and to play it to the end. If you do not confirm, do not come, or leave a ranked match, you have to wait before you can search again, and leaving a match also costs rating; repeated leaving makes the waiting longer.

To keep matches fair our servers check what the game sends, keep records of suspicious behaviour and read the reports that players send about each other. If these show that an account cheated, we may remove it from ranked play or ban it for a period of time or for good, and we may do this automatically. A ban applies to the account. If you think a ban is a mistake, write to us at outworldinteractive@gmail.com and we will look at it. Sending false or abusive reports about other players is also against the rules. When you delete your account your level, rank and ranked record are erased and cannot be restored.
